# Reel Vault — Product Context

**register: product**

## Product Purpose

A personal, single-user archive for everything worth keeping from Instagram:
saved reels and posts, and direct-message threads (with the reels shared inside
them). It ingests the user's official Instagram data export, enriches items with
AI summaries and tags, and makes the whole hoard browsable and searchable in one
calm place. It is a private library, not a social feed.

## Users

One person: the owner. Power user of Instagram who saves far more than they ever
revisit. They open this in the evening, often on a phone in bed, sometimes a
laptop, to actually find that one editing tutorial or recipe they saved months
ago, or to reread a conversation. Mood is relaxed retrieval, not urgent work.

## Brand & Tone

Quiet, warm, archival. Feels like a well-kept personal notebook or a library
reading room, not a dashboard and not a social app. Confident and editorial:
generous type, real hierarchy, calm surfaces. Trustworthy with private data
(DMs live here). Never loud, never gamified, no engagement bait.

## Strategic Principles

- Retrieval over feed: search and browse are the point; no infinite scroll dopamine loop.
- The content is the hero; chrome recedes.
- Private by default: passcode-gated, single user, data stays in the user's own backend.
- Honest about limits: ingest is from the official export, not a live magic sync.

## Anti-references (do NOT look like these)

- Instagram itself: no magenta-to-orange gradient, no neon, no story rings, no glossy gradients.
- Generic dark SaaS dashboards: no navy + electric-blue, no glowing cards, no hero-metric template.
- Crypto/AI neon-on-black.
- Same-sized icon+heading+text card grids repeated endlessly.
